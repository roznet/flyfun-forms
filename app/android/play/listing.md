## Full description (≤4000)

FlyFun Forms fills in the customs, immigration and airport forms that general
aviation flights need, from the flight, crew and passengers you already have.

Enter a flight once: route, times, aircraft, who is on board. The app finds
the forms each airport asks for and fills them in, ready to email or upload.

• Airport-specific forms: an airport's or authority's own customs and
  immigration PDFs, such as the French Préavis Douane
• Upload files: the UK General Aviation Report (GAR) and handling requests as
  spreadsheets
• Web forms: for airports that only take an online form, the app opens the
  page and pre-fills it
• General Declaration (GenDec) for any airport without a dedicated form
• Customs email addresses from the AIP, and emails written in the airport's
  language when you speak it

Fast to set up
• Scan a passport's machine-readable zone with the camera; the image is never
  saved
• Import people from your contacts, and flights from FlyFun Weather
• Reuse the crew and passengers of a previous flight

Your data stays on your phone
People, documents, aircraft and flights are stored only on this device, and
are not backed up to Google. The server fills in each form and keeps nothing:
no names, passport numbers or flight details are stored. Move your data to
another phone with an encrypted file only you can open.

Filling forms requires a free FlyFun account (Google or Apple sign-in).
Entering data works without one.

Privacy policy: https://forms.flyfun.aero/privacy

- New flights now start from a single Import button, with four ways to begin: paste an ICAO flight plan, repeat a previous flight, or import from FlyFun Weather or Autorouter
- Import a route from Autorouter: pick from your recent routes, once your Autorouter account is linked in the FlyFun Weather web app
- The + on the Flights list now opens the new flight form straight away
- New flights start on the aircraft you last flew, and you can copy the crew from an earlier flight in one tap
- Redesigned departure and arrival time entry: pick the date, hour, minute and time zone, shown in the airport's local time by default
- Switching time zone now shows the same moment in the new zone, and daylight saving is worked out for the flight's own date
- Fixed flight plans being read incorrectly, which could fill the form with the wrong route or leave the date and time unchanged
- Fixed arrivals after midnight being set a day early, and times sometimes appearing in the wrong time zone
